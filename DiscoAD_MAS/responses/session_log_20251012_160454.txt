> 💡 Why survival analysis?

## 🔬 Step 3: Advanced Analyses – Robustness, Moderation, and Mechanism Exploration

**Objective**: Strengthen conclusions through deeper investigation into mechanisms and variability across subgroups.

### Actions:
1. **Mediation Analysis** *(Optional but powerful)*  
   - Test whether **cognitive performance (MoCA/MMSE)** mediates the relationship between education and dementia.  
   - Use Baron & Kenny’s method or structural equation modeling (SEM):  
     - Path 1: Education → MoCA  
     - Path 2: Education → Dementia  
     - Path 3: MoCA → Dementia  
   - Estimate indirect effects (mediated impact).  
   - Interpret: “Education improves cognitive function, which in turn delays dementia.”

2. **Moderation Analysis**  
   - Explore whether the benefit of education differs by:  
     - APOE genotype (especially e4 carriers)  
     - Sex (women vs men)  
     - Pre-existing cardiovascular conditions (hypertension/stroke/depression)  
   - Add interaction terms:  
     ```stata
     Years_of_education × APOE_e4_1, Years_of_education × sex, etc.
     ```
   - Test significance (p < 0.05) and interpret directionality.  
   - Example: "The protective effect of education is stronger in non-e4 carriers."

3. **Subgroup Sensitivity Analysis**  
   - Re-run Cox models stratified by:  
     - Age (<65, ≥65)  
     - Sex  
     - Presence of vascular comorbidity (stroke/hypertension)  
   - Assess consistency of results across groups.

> 🔄 These analyses help determine if the effect is generalizable or conditional on biology/environmental factors.
