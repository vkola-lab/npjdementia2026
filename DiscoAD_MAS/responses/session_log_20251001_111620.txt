#### 2. **Primary Analysis: Rank-Based Interaction Model**

##### A. **Slope Comparison Using Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test (Within-Subject)**


- Quantile regression allows estimation of median trajectories without assuming normality
- Enables comparison of median slopes across groups

##### C. **Multiple Comparison Correction**
