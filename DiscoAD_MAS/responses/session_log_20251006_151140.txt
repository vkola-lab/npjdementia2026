**Method: Cox Proportional Hazards Model**
```plaintext
h(t | X) = h₀(t) × exp(β₁×age + β₂×sex + ... )
```

**Variables:**
- Time-to-event: Time from baseline to dementia onset (if longitudinal), or **censoring based on visit date**.
- Event indicator: `Met_criteria_for_dementia == 1`, otherwise censored.

**Critical Assumptions to Validate:**
- Proportional hazards assumption → tested via Schoenfeld residuals and scaled Schoenfeld residual plots.
- Time-varying covariates not required unless follow-up periods vary significantly.

**Outputs:**
- Hazard ratio (HR) for age (per year increase); interpret as relative instantaneous risk.
- 95% confidence intervals for HR.

- Comparison with logistic regression results (e.g., HR ≈ OR under certain conditions).

**Advantage Over Logistic Model:**
- More suitable when outcomes occur over time and not just at a single point.
