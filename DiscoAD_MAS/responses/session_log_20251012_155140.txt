**Approach: Cox Proportional Hazards Regression**

  $$  
  Where `h(t)` is the hazard of developing dementia at time `t`.

- **Cox model justification**:
  - Dementia is a rare event with long follow-up times → survival analysis is ideal.
  - It accounts for time-to-event nature and allows estimation of hazard ratios (HRs) with confidence intervals.
  - Adjusts for confounders (age, gender, APOE genotype, vascular risk factors, etc.).

- **Key outputs**:

# Confidence interval for coefficient (using bootstrap method due to small sample size limitations)

    boot_coef = []
    for _ in range(n_bootstraps):

        X_boot = data.iloc[idx]
